My Ethos on AI

I am no expert in AI but I do know there are two primary types of AI that are discussed in the modern day: Analytical and Generative. As far as analytical AI is concerned, I believe it is an extremely helpful tool that streamlines and quickens many processes while increasing their accuracy, when proper training and testing is done. However, analytical AI seems to not be a strong point of contention, that goes to generative AI.

Art

Generative AI has a lot of discourse surrounding it and I think it's important that people have an opinion to weigh in on the issue with; Mine is as follows: Generative AI has no place in the creative fields, those being art, writing, music production, film-making, and all other related fields. Generative AI, like analytical AI, is a tool, but it is important to not ball peen hammer on nails.

Programming

Generative AI is a very helpful tool in coding but it is also extremely dangerous to one's ability as a programmer. LLMs should never be used to code something that you yourself cannot code. I think it is incredibly important that AI only be used in coding to save time and not to supplement your knowledge. For example, when writing the database models for this website I employed AI to generate the python classes. I know how to actually do this, but it saves time because its not a lightning fast process to type out "library_ID: so.Mapped[int] = so.mapped_column(sa.Integer, primary_key=True)" several times. But if I need to do something I've never done before and don't know how to do, I sit down, read documentation till I hate it, write bad code, debug it, and repeat that process until I've written a solid verison of what I had envisioned.

Research and Learning

If you need to do actualy research: No. Close Claude, gpt, gemini, or whatever model you would use and open your preferrd internet browser. Proper research requires you to find reliable sources and read the whole story they present. Seriously, if you are using AI to do your research for you, then I immediately distrust your take and think less of your ability to collect information. As for learning, my opinion is largely the same. However, I do think LLMs can play a role is learning when deployed properly. Let me clarify: I do NOT think AI should be used in the classroom(Teachers exist for a reason), but I find that AI can be helpful for studying and prcticing outside the classroom(e.g. generating practice problems that test specific areas)
